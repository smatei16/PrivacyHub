HaveIBeenPwned API Key

Stored locally and only sent to haveibeenpwned.com. Get a Core subscription key at haveibeenpwned.com/API/Key. The Core plan is sufficient — email lookup is not required.

Cached Analyses

Privacy policy analyses and breach check results are cached per domain. Breach results are refreshed automatically after 7 days. Clear the cache to force a fresh analysis or breach check on next use.

How It Works

PrivacyHub scans the current page for links to privacy policy and terms of service pages, fetches their content, and sends it to Claude for analysis. You receive a privacy score, a list of personal data collected, third-party vendors, and a plain-language summary.
  • 1 Visit any website — PrivacyHub automatically detects policy links and checks HIBP for data breaches.
  • 2 The Data Breach Check section shows instantly: green if safe, red/amber if the site has known breaches with affected data types and a password recommendation.
  • 3 Click the extension icon and press Analyze Privacy Policy to get an AI-powered privacy score, summary, and vendor list.
  • 4 All results are cached — revisiting the same site shows results instantly. Breach data refreshes after 7 days.
